NEON FIGHTING · MVP
Turn your phone horizontal · headphones on

HOW TO PLAY

1 PLAYER

D-PAD — move. Up to jump, down to crouch.

PUNCH — fast strike. Whiffs on a crouching opponent.

KICK — heavy strike. Breaks crouch.

BLOCK — hold to defend. Does not work in the air.

SPECIAL — unique attack. Costs 50 energy.

COMBO — PUNCH→PUNCH “Double”, PUNCH→PUNCH→KICK “Flurry”, KICK→PUNCH “Switch”. Land the first hit to continue.

COMMANDS — F,F+PUNCH Rush, F,F+KICK Charge, D,F+PUNCH Uppercut, D,B+KICK Sweep, F,D,B+ATTACK Breaker.

VOLT — plasma. BLAZE — shockwave. NYX — dash. MIRA — shard fan. STORM — lightning.

2 PLAYERS

The screen splits in half: each player gets a D-pad and buttons.

Player 1 is on the left, player 2 on the right. Colors follow the chosen fighter.

Each player picks a fighter on the P1 or P2 tab. Pick the same one for a mirror match.

KEYBOARD

P1: A D W S + F punch, G kick, H block, T special.

P2: ← → ↑ ↓ + 1 punch, 2 kick, 3 block, 4 special.

Energy builds when you hit or take damage. First to two round wins takes the fight.
